### Added

- `/gpt-account unblock <id>` lifts a usage-limit block on a ChatGPT Subscription account, mirroring `/claude-account unblock`: after a provider-side reset the account is usable again without removing and re-adding it. An auth block, model-scoped limits and an in-flight lease are left in place. Reported by CodeRiever ([#4603](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/4603))
