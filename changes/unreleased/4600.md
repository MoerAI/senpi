### Added

- `/gpt-account unblock <id>` lifts a usage-limit block on a ChatGPT Subscription account, mirroring `/claude-account unblock`: after a provider-side reset the account is usable again without removing and re-adding it. An auth block still needs a login. Reported by CodeRiever ([#4600](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/issues/4600))
