## 2026-11-14 - Relative and home monitor targets stay local (senpi#6243)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/rpc-monitor-targets.ts`: `isRemoteShape` no longer reads a target whose first segment starts with `.` or `~` (`./s`, `../s`, `~/s`, `.omo/s`) as `<device>/<session>`, and matches the `.jsonl` suffix in any case. Those targets parse as `{ kind: "id" }` again; `<device>/<session>` and `<device>/*` are unchanged.

### Why

- Since the remote target case (senpi#6151), a relative or home session path with one `/` was refused as a session on another device, or handed to a registered remote source (senpi#6243).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The target grammar is the terminal builtin's own parse; a remote source only serves targets this parse already classified as remote.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `isRemoteShape` and the constants above `parseRpcTarget`.

### Fixed

- `monitor` rpc targets such as `./s`, `../s`, `~/s`, `.omo/s` and `dir/s.JSONL` resolve as local session paths again instead of being refused as sessions on another device ([#6243](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/issues/6243)).
