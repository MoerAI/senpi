## 2026-10-27 - Kimi provider defaults move to K3.1 Pro (senpi#4443)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/model-resolver.ts`: `defaultModelPerProvider` picks Kimi K3.1 Pro where the provider lists it: `opengateway` `moonshotai/kimi-k3` -> `moonshotai/kimi-k3.1-pro`, `opencode-go` `kimi-k3` -> `kimi-k3.1-pro`, `moonshotai` and `moonshotai-cn` `kimi-k2.6` -> `kimi-k3.1-pro`. Fireworks and Together stay on K3 because their catalogs list no K3.1 Pro.

### Why

- Moonshot's current flagship is K3.1 Pro, and Moonshot stops offering K2 models to newly registered accounts on 2026-10-31 (senpi#4441).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `defaultModelPerProvider` is the core fallback table consulted before any extension chooses a model.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `defaultModelPerProvider` object literal in `model-resolver.ts`.

## 2026-10-27 - Kimi recommendations and the look-at chain move to K3.1 Pro (senpi#4443)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/recommended-models/index.ts`: the Kimi rung of `RECOMMENDED_DEFAULT_MODELS` is `kimi-k3.1-pro` at `max` (was `kimi-k3`); `canonicalModelId` maps Kimi Code's bare `k3.1-pro` to `kimi-k3.1-pro` as it maps `k3` to `kimi-k3`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/look-at/model-selector.ts`: `DEFAULT_LOOK_AT_CHAIN` ends at `kimi-k3.1-pro` (was `kimi-k3`).

### Why

- Kimi K3.1 Pro replaces K3 as Moonshot's flagship (senpi#4441); implicit Kimi picks should land on it.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- These are the builtin extensions' own shipped defaults.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `RECOMMENDED_DEFAULT_MODELS` table and `DEFAULT_LOOK_AT_CHAIN`.
