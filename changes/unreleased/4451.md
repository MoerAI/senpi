## 2026-10-27 - CLI-store account slots with per-account config dirs (senpi#4436, S3)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/accounts.ts`: `AccountSlot` is now `LegacyAccountSlot | ClaudeCliAccountSlot`, discriminated by `store`. A `claude-cli` slot holds no token: `dirId` names its account dir, plus optional `signedInAt`, `profile`, `check` and `signInRequired`. Token-holding helpers (`upsertAccount`, `refreshSlot`, `isSentinelSlot`, `envSlots`) act only on legacy slots.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/claude-cli-account-dir.ts` (new): `accountDirFor` (`<agentDir>/anthropic-subscription-claude/<32 hex>`), `ensureAccountDir` (0700 dir, creates or repairs the `CLAUDE_CLI_SHARED_LINKS` links to the shared root, junctions on Windows), and `removeAccountDir` (`claude auth logout` for that dir, then unlink without following and remove; a failed sign-out throws and keeps everything).
- `auth-lane.ts`, `auth-block-recovery.ts`, `failover.ts`, `config-dir-credentials.ts`, `oauth-login.ts`, `session-registry.ts`: narrowed to the legacy member. A `claude-cli` slot that reaches the token lane fails `authentication_failed` until S4 gives it its own lane.

### Why

- senpi#4436 moves Claude subscription sign-in to the bundled CLI's own store. Each account needs its own config dir so the CLI keys its credential store per account, while transcripts and user settings stay shared through links. Removal must sign the account out of the CLI store first, or the store entry would be orphaned.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The slot shape and account dir belong to this builtin provider's account lifecycle, which the core account listing and removal call into.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `AccountSlot` type in `accounts.ts`; the narrowing lines in `auth-lane.ts` `prepareSlot`/`continueWhileStoreBusy` and `failover.ts` `persistBlock`.

## 2026-10-27 - Claude CLI-store accounts in the account listing and removal (senpi#4436, S3)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-accounts.ts`: `removeCredentialAccount` signs a `claude-cli` slot out (`removeAccountDir`, resolved `claude`, agent dir from the auth path) before the slot is dropped; a failed sign-out throws and keeps the account. `CredentialAccountSummary` gains optional `store`, `signedInAt`, `email`, `orgName`, `subscriptionType`, `check` and `signIn`, read defensively from stored slots.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/subscription-limits/usage-accounts.ts`: the usage poller skips `claude-cli` slots (no token to poll with); `anthropic-oauth-migration.ts` matches grants only against token-holding slots.

### Why

- Clients need the email, plan and sign-in state to render an account row, and removal must not orphan the CLI's credential store entry (senpi#4436).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Account listing and removal are core credential surfaces shared by every provider.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `summarizeInStoredOrder` summary literal and `removeCredentialAccount` in `credential-accounts.ts`.

## 2026-10-27 - get_provider_accounts carries Claude CLI-store account fields (senpi#4436, S3)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-session-state-types.ts`: `RpcProviderAccount` gains optional `store: "claude-cli"`, `signedInAt`, `email`, `orgName`, `subscriptionType`, `check` and `signIn: "signed_in" | "needs_sign_in"`. `source` stays `"login"`.

### Why

- The desktop account row shows the email and plan and a sign-in-again state (senpi#4436). The fields are optional, so older clients keep decoding the payload.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The RPC wire type is owned by the RPC mode.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the end of the `RpcProviderAccount` interface.
